I am a Principal Applied Scientist at Microsoft with experience leading AI safety initiatives that translate emerging risks into practical policy and design guidance. I led responsible AI red teaming for several frontier models using a multi-stakeholder, interdisciplinary approach that brought together experts from product, policy, and several fields of research, helping to expand red teaming beyond traditional security testing to address broader responsible AI and societal risks and to establish practical guidelines for doing so. I currently co-lead a cross-company initiative on the psychological influences of conversational AI, bringing together research, product, and policy perspectives to safeguard user wellbeing and promote healthy human--AI interaction.
